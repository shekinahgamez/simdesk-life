Quick answers

What does it cost?

Free to start. The full app is $8 a month billed annually ($96), or $10 month to month, with a 14-day free trial and no card. Beta testers get the first year free.

Is it a mod?

No. Simdesk is a website you keep open next to the game. The Simdesk Logger is a separate, optional mod that logs your household and makes updating easy.

Do I have to type everything?

No. Use the optional Logger, or type it yourself with the Ctrl K command bar.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Desktop gets the command bar. Phone gets a tap-only Quick actions menu.

Can I take my data with me?

Always. Backup file, spreadsheet or photos zip.
