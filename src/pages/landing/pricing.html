Quick answers

Does Casual stay free?+

Yes. No trial, no time limit.

How does the free trial work?+

You get 14 days of Obsessed with no card. We email you 7 days before it ends. If you do nothing, you drop to Casual.

What if I go back to Casual?+

Everything you made stays, and you can export it any time. Obsessed features become read-only. The one exception is photos past 100: you have 30 days to download them, and we email you until you do.

What do beta testers get?+

The first year of Obsessed free after release.
