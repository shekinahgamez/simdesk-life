## Done
- Fewer clicks (boards 1 to 10): tap a household cell to pick one, Select mode for moving Sims in bulk, relationship picker with housemates first and Save and add another, tap a profile row to edit it with Undo, Next day / Jump ahead / Go back, Pick a home lists the game's lots, compact Sim profile, Today page instead of the deck, smaller world and household page controls, tighter club page with a pencil per card
- Today page (replaces the home deck)
- Rotation: Playing now card on Today, Rotation page (order by dragging, turn length per household, Play now / Play next, take out), End turn (never moves the date), a line on the household page, a Playing now tag on Sim profiles
- Phase 3: Sims
- Worlds & lots (built before Households, since homes need lots)
- Phase 4: Households (in the Sims tab: household cards, household page, picking a home, move out or merge, who owns what, rental units)
- Calendar (season banners; holidays, events, birthdays and vacations; Coming up on Sim profiles and household pages)
- Real-life ages (per-save switch, ages set in App settings, shown on profile, cards and list)
- Maps out, world scenes in: the AI maps and "Use my map" are gone; 18 world scenes on the Worlds tab and as the world page banner; neighborhood cards (your lots in bold, open spots gray with a +); the lot rename gap fixed (renaming a lot type updates lists right away)
- Photos area: its own section (no deck card) with a grid, a photo page, caption, Sims tagged (Sims only), a game date and/or the day it was added (game date leads, added day is the quiet line), photos on calendar items and birthdays, and a Photos card on Sim profiles
- Calendar, round 2: themed headers (24 holiday and 9 event pictures to pick from, plus vacation, getaway and birthday headers), a birthday page, vacation or getaway with an optional place to stay, your own lot types (like Hotel), and one season calendar picker for every date in the app
- Photos filter: plain field rows (Sims, When, Occasion, Club, Sort), a list behind each, a game-date range on the calendar picker; the photo viewer follows the grid you opened it from
- Clubs (Get Together only): list, club page, one form with a list behind each pick. A club has members and a leader (Child and older, up to 3 clubs each), who can join (open or invite only), where it meets (a lot, a lot type, or nowhere in particular), up to 5 encouraged and 5 banned activities (each can be aimed at another club), up to 5 requirements, perks, notes and photos. A Clubs card on Sim profiles; photos can be tagged with a club

## Later
- **Clubs, more**: club points and gatherings, a club's logo, and the game's premade clubs were left out on purpose (the owner doesn't want them). Revisit only if asked.
- **Photos, more**: bulk upload (pick many photos at once, shared defaults, a progress count, stops at the photo limit); a photo's own season/year view; tagging lots or worlds only if people ask for it.
- **Career job titles**: show "Administrative Assistant" instead of "Business · Level 2" (needs every career's level names, checked against the game).
- **Mod switches**: say which common mods you play with (for example a more-traits mod), only where a mod changes how SimDesk works.
- **"Log Sims for SimDesk" mod**: a small in-game mod that writes a file of the Sims you pick; uploading it adds them all with traits, ages and households.
- **Lumpinou's Sim log**: read that mod's file too.
- **Households, more**: phone actions menu (⋯) on the household page; World and Homeless filters on the Sims list.
- **Lot ownership rules (needs lot types)**: no mods: a household has one home lot plus any community lots. For Rent: one home plus any number of residential rental lots. Real Estate Mod by Sim Realist: any number of residential lots and rental lots.
- **Pricing tiers**: what paid accounts get (more photos and so on). Limits already live in one place per plan.
- **Penthouse spots**: mark which spots are penthouses. Any lot type except Apartment and Residential rental can go there.
- **World scenes as banners**: redraw each world scene once on a wide canvas, so the world page banner shows the whole width and the Worlds tab tile shows the centered middle (keep the main subject centered). One drawing covers both sizes; no zooming in on tile art. Test first with Willow Creek and Brindleton Bay, then widen the other drawn worlds. Draw the remaining worlds wide from the start. Drafts so far are in design/world-scenes.

- **Preteen stage** (for the preteen mod): real-life ages 10 to 11, teen 12 and up.
- **Festivals on the calendar**: only if simmers ask for it a lot.
- **Events at a lot**: only if simmers ask (kept off so adding an event never feels like a chore).
